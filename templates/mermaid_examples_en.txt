# Problem solving in four steps
flowchart TD
  A[Understand the task] --> B[Make a plan]
  B --> C[Carry out the plan]
  C --> D{Does the result make sense?}
  D -->|Yes| E[Look back: what have I learnt?]
  D -->|No| B

# Water cycle

  D --> E[Lakes and seas]
  E --> A

# Food chain
flowchart LR
  S[Sun] --> P[Plant]
  P --> H[Herbivore]
  H --> F[Carnivore]
  F --> Z[Decomposers]
  P --> Z
  H --> Z
  Z -->|Nutrients| P

# Inquiry-based learning
flowchart TD
  A[Ask a question] --> B[Make a hypothesis]
  B --> C[Plan an experiment]
  C --> D[Carry out the experiment]
  D --> E[Analyse the observations]
  E --> F{Hypothesis confirmed?}
  F -->|Yes| G[Present the result]
  F -->|No| B

# Kolb's learning cycle
flowchart LR
  A[Concrete experience] --> B[Reflective observation]
  B --> C[Forming concepts and rules]
  C --> D[Active experimentation]
  D --> A

# Checking a source
flowchart TD
  A[New information] --> B{Is the author known?}
  B -->|No| X[Careful: look for further sources]
  B -->|Yes| C{Up to date?}
  C -->|No| X
  C -->|Yes| D{Confirmed by other sources?}
  D -->|No| X
  D -->|Yes| E[Use and cite the source]

# Writing process
flowchart LR
  A[Plan] --> B[Write a draft]
  B --> C[Get feedback]
  C --> D[Revise]
  D --> E{Finished?}
  E -->|No| C
  E -->|Yes| F[Publish]

# Separation of powers
flowchart TD
  V((People)) -->|elect| L[Parliament: passes laws]
  L -->|elects or controls| E[Government: implements laws]
  L -->|elects| J[Courts: administer justice]
  J -->|review| E

# Peer feedback
sequenceDiagram
  participant A as Learner A
  participant B as Learner B
  A->>B: Shows the draft
  B-->>A: This works well
  B-->>A: One suggestion for improvement
  A->>A: Revises the draft
  A->>B: Shows the new version

# Sender and receiver
sequenceDiagram
  participant S as Sender
  participant E as Receiver
  S->>E: Message (encoded in words and gestures)
  Note right of E: decodes and interprets
  E-->>S: Feedback
  S->>E: Clears up misunderstandings
